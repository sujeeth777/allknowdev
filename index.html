WHY IT'S FREENO ADS · NO TRACKING

allknowdev is a public good, not a funnel.

No affiliate links. No paid placements. No dark patterns. The data lives in plain JSON files in the repository — anyone can audit, correct or extend it. If a service changes its free tier, a PR fixes it for everyone within minutes.

⚠ Prices change without notice. The free tier you read about today may differ tomorrow. Every entry carries its check date and a direct link to the official pricing page — treat this as a map, not gospel.
