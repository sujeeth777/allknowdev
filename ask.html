Submitting opens GitHub in a new tab with your message prefilled — you stay in control of what gets posted. Anonymous is fine.

What I can help with

🧭 Choosing what to build or learn next

Tell me your background and goals — you'll get a specific, opinionated plan mapped to free resources in this atlas, not a generic roadmap dump.

🏗️ Reviewing your stack before you commit

About to build on a free tier? I'll sanity-check the limits against your expected usage and warn you where projects usually outgrow them.

🆓 Finding a free option for X

"Is there a free alternative to ___?" is the most common question — the directory probably has it, and if it doesn't, I'll research one and add it.

🌎 Region-specific blockers

Payment declines, unavailable services, verification loops — describe what's happening in your country and I'll point to what actually works.

⏱ Response expectations

Best-effort and usually within a few days. This is a public thread — good questions and answers stay visible so the next person with the same problem finds them.
